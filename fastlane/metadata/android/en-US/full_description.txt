Pi-hole Connect is the best way to connect to and manage your Pi-hole®

Features:
- Toggling Pi-hole® on/off with timer
- Quick statistics
- Detailed logs
- Control & modify black/white list
- Fully open-source for your peace of mind

*This is a third-party application. The Pi-hole team and the development of the Pi-hole software is not related in any way with this application.

*Only support Pi-hole version 6.0 or higher.
